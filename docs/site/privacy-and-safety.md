# Privacy and safety

Nearer is a private place for one family. Here is what that means.

## What we keep

Your family's tree, the photos, voice memories, recipes, stories and care notes you share, and the settings you choose. Files sit in private storage.

## Who can see what

- Only the people in your family. Every read and write is locked to your family.
- Nothing crosses between homes unless someone shares it.
- Other members' emails and the internal person ID never leave the server.
- Nothing is public unless you make a share link. A share link is an opt-in, frozen copy you can revoke.
- No advertising, no selling your data, no tracking across apps.

If you are signed in, find the controls in Settings → Privacy.

## Safe & sound: only what you share

Safe & sound shows only what each person chooses to share, and photos lose their location before upload.

- Opening the app, the lamp, and the clock are never read as “all good”, “asleep” or “available”.
- Nothing is inferred from silence.
- The quiet-morning note exists only if a parent turns it on, and goes to the one person they chose.
- Calls start only after a tap on a consent screen. Audio-only is always offered.

Safety check-ins:

- You can share “Heading home”, “Travelling”, or “I need help”.
- A check-in with location hides the location after 24 hours on read.
- An open check-in stays open for 48 hours, then closes as “no reply” and the location is dropped.
- You can close a check-in yourself, or start a new one.

## Sessions and sign-in limits

- Sessions use httpOnly, SameSite=Lax, HMAC-signed cookies.
- Sign-in by email code is rate-limited.
- Invite codes expire in 7 days.
- Some actions are rate-limited. Limits are best-effort and slide over time.

## What Muse may and may not do

Muse is the family's helper, never a family member. Meta's AI models write captions, transcripts and translations for your family.

| Muse may | Muse may not |
|---|---|
| Write down a voice memory, recipe, story or request | Post as a person, or post anything automatically |
| Translate what someone said or wrote | Chat in place of your children, or give medical advice |
| Describe what is visible in a photo | Invent people or events, or add ingredients that were not said |
| Suggest a caption, recipe card, question or plan for a person to review | Keep anything without a person approving it |
| Paint or restore a photo only after a person previews it and taps Keep | Clone voices, or use face recognition |

More guardrails:

- Everything Muse makes is seen by a person before the family sees it.
- Anything Muse painted or restored says so in the image and in every viewer — “Painted by Muse” or “Restored by Muse”.
- Portraits are always painted style, from your family's own photos only.
- Teens and children never start a portrait.

Your voice stays in the family. Voice memories are heard by your family, nobody else. Voices are never cloned, and there is no face recognition.

## Delete anytime and export

- A grown-up can download the family's data as one file. Export strips emails, Muse logs and safety locations.
- You can delete your reactions, wishes and listens.
- If you leave the family, your profile and place in the tree are deleted. Memories you shared stay with the family.

Some things clear by themselves:

| What clears | When |
|---|---|
| Check-in location | Hidden after 24 hours, dropped if there is no reply |
| Open check-in | Closed as “no reply” after 48 hours |
| Closed check-ins | Deleted after 30 days |
| Heartbeats and moments | Deleted after 15 days |
| Muse call log | Deleted after 90 days |
